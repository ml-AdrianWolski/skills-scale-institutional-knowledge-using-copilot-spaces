# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

Welcome to the OctoAcme Project Management documentation hub. This folder contains our standardized approaches to project planning, execution, release, and continuous improvement across all OctoAcme initiatives.

## Overview

OctoAcme follows a structured lifecycle approach to project management, ensuring consistency, transparency, and quality across all teams. Our framework is inspired by industry best practices and tailored to fit OctoAcme's collaborative culture.

### Project Management Lifecycle Stages

- **Initiation** – Define the project scope, objectives, and stakeholders. Establish the business case and secure approvals to proceed.
- **Planning** – Develop the project plan, assign resources, set milestones, and identify dependencies and risks.
- **Execution & Tracking** – Implement the project plan, track progress against milestones, and manage day-to-day activities.
- **Risks & Communication** – Monitor, assess, and mitigate risks throughout the project. Maintain clear and consistent communication with all stakeholders.
- **Release & Deployment** – Coordinate and execute the release process, ensuring readiness across engineering, QA, and operations.
- **Retrospective & Continuous Improvement** – Review completed work to capture lessons learned and drive ongoing improvements to processes and practices.
- **Roles & Personas** – Define team roles, responsibilities, and personas to ensure clear ownership and accountability.
